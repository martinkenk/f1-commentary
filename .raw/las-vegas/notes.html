Las Vegas result and Qatar carry-over

Max Verstappen won from Lando Norris (+0.639s) and Oscar Piastri (+1.529s). Antonelli qualified first but did not start on pole after his PU sanction. Official race classification · All session tables and actual starting grid.

Unresolved FIA classification footnote mismatch: Stewards' Document 54 orders a 10-second penalty for Lewis Hamilton after leaving the track at Turn 8 and retaining an advantage. The later Document 65 final classification lists Hamilton seventh at +12.795s but names only the Leclerc and Hadjar penalties. Formula1.com's race report also says Hamilton received the 10-second penalty; both it and Document 65 place him seventh. The missing Document 65 footnote remains unexplained, so no corrected order is inferred. Document 54 · Document 65 · Formula1.com race report.

FP1 finished with no timed laps in wet conditions: installation running is not a classification. Official FP1 report.

Qatar watch: Stewards' Documents 55 and 56 summoned Sergio Perez (Car 11) and Rafael Câmara (Car 51), respectively, over the alleged Turn 14 incident at 20:33; summonses are allegations, not rulings. The later Document 61 decision found Perez wholly responsible. Because he retired and could not serve the 10-second time penalty, it became a five-place drop at his next race. Doc 55: Perez summons · Doc 56: Câmara summons · Doc 61: Stewards' ruling · Qatar briefing.

Both 2026 championships are settled

Kimi Antonelli clinched the Drivers' Championship with third place in Mexico City on 1 November. Mercedes secured the Constructors' title in São Paulo on 8 November, where George Russell finished fourth and Antonelli fifth. The Las Vegas weekend is no longer a title-decider; focus on race wins and the remaining finishing positions. Formula1.com on Antonelli's title · Formula1.com on Mercedes' title.

McLaren's Las Vegas package is now documented

The FIA car-presentation filing lists two McLaren floor-furniture items: a new floor board and a floor-edge change, both described as performance flow-conditioning work. Andrea Stella's earlier interview previewed more parts for Las Vegas but did not identify them. That interview is a team statement and was not an FIA filing. The event filing is evidence of declared parts, not proof of a measured lap-time gain. FIA team submissions · Formula1.com, 9 November.

The circuit in one paragraph

A cold, late-night, very low-downforce blast down the Strip. Enormous top speeds and almost no corners of consequence — the whole weekend is defined by the struggle to get tyres into their working range on a cold street surface.

Name-check these corners

  • Turn 1 — Tight left immediately after the start; first-lap chaos point.
  • Turns 5–7 — The slow sequence before the Strip — traction out of here defines the long straight.
  • Turn 14 — End of the Strip; the heaviest braking zone and the primary overtaking place.
