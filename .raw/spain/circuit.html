Straight Mode is the active-aero system, not the electrical Overtake allowance. The map places Overtake detection at entry to T22 and activation 20 m after T22. The PU sheet identifies loops L24/L25 but still marks their absolute lap distances TBC.

Sector and timing references

  • Map sectors: 1.839 / 2.049 / 1.526 km.
  • Intermediate 1: 85 m before T7; intermediate 2: 40 m before T16.
  • Speed trap: 160 m before T5.

Track limits and escape routes

  • Turn 22: during lap-time classified sessions, failing to negotiate the corner can lead the Stewards to invalidate the current and immediately following lap.
  • T1/T2 runoff: a car missing T2 to the right of the yellow line must stay right of it and rejoin at the far end, after T2.
  • T5/T5A runoff: stay right of the yellow line and rejoin at T6.
  • Inside T11: rejoin to the left of the polystyrene block.

Practice starts and the narrow pit lane

  • No practice starts at the normal pit exit or during qualifying.
  • Cars on track at the FP2 end signal may complete two additional laps, solely to make a grid practice start on each.
  • Race reconnaissance has a specific practice-start area before Safety Car Line 2 on the pit-exit road; the separate routing in paragraph 13.6 applies.
  • During the race, a released car must safely merge into the fast lane as soon as possible, slowing to let existing fast-lane traffic pass if needed. All parts of the car must cross the separating line.

Race-control details

  • The SC2-to-SC1 maximum time for qualifying and race reconnaissance will be communicated after FP2; Document 5 gives no numeric maximum yet.
  • A double-yellow sector in free practice means deletion of that lap time.
  • Blue-flag pre-warning: 3.0 s; blue panels/cockpit lights: 1.2 s.
  • After a suspension the Safety Car leaves the pits one minute before resumption and waits before T18.
  • Turn 12 light panels are mirrored; panel 6 has two physical panels, and panel 11 is mirrored on panel 10.

Operational rules: FIA Document 5, Race Director's Competition Notes, 10 September, paragraphs 1, 2, 9, 13, 16, 22, 25–27. These are instructions, not individual stewards' decisions.
