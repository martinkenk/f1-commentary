Madrid: FIA facts to have ready

  • Two Straight Mode zones: after T22 and after T3. The separate Overtake detection is at entry to T22, with activation 20 m after T22.
  • Qualifying recharge: 7.5 MJ/lap, versus 9.0 MJ in free practice. Race recharge is 8.5 MJ without Overtake and 9.0 MJ with it.
  • C2 / C3 / C4 are confirmed. Slick starting pressures are 26.5 psi front / 25.5 psi rear in FIA/Pirelli Issue A.
  • T22 can cost two lap times; the SC2-to-SC1 maximum lap time is to be communicated after FP2, not a number to guess in advance.

FIA Documents 1, 3, 5 and 6, checked 10 September 2026. Circuit & rules / Power unit / Tyres.

Confirmed race-driver replacements

  • Liam Lawson continues alongside Max Verstappen at Red Bull for a third weekend.
  • Yuki Tsunoda continues alongside Arvid Lindblad at Racing Bulls.
  • Isack Hadjar remains out while recovering from his wrist injury. The team says recovery is progressing and he will support them in Madrid; no return date is asserted here.
  • These are race-seat substitutions, not announcements of mandatory rookie FP1 outings.

Formula1.com / Red Bull announcement, 7 September 2026.

The form carried into Madrid

  • Mercedes: Antonelli arrives after winning at Monza from P19; the new venue tests both drivers' adaptation rather than a known circuit-specific form line.
  • Ferrari: Hamilton and Leclerc's opening-lap Monza fight and Leclerc's separate crash frame the team's response; the published preview discusses team-order questions, not an announced new policy.
  • McLaren: Norris beat Piastri to P4 at Monza after they were allowed to race. The previous Hungary and Zandvoort wins do not guarantee the same pace here.
  • Red Bull: Verstappen arrives from a Monza podium, with Lawson continuing to substitute.
  • Alpine / Racing Bulls: Gasly's Monza pole became P7 in the race, ahead of Lindblad, Colapinto and Tsunoda. Madrid's new layout gives no historical basis to carry over Alpine's Monza advantage.

What the circuit demands

Brand new for 2026 — a part-street, part-permanent lap around the IFEMA exhibition grounds, including a banked corner. There is no previous Grand Prix here to benchmark: the FIA's operating limits are published, while measured degradation and strategy depend on this weekend's running.

Weekend format

Conventional weekend — three practice sessions, Qualifying and the Grand Prix.

  • All session times are shown in Madrid (CEST) and Tallinn (EEST).
  • Live weather is attached to each session on the Schedule & Weather page.
  • Session results appear on the Results page as soon as each session finishes.

Storylines to prepare

  • Anything can be called a 'first' here — first pole, first lap record, first winner.
  • Watch for track evolution: green, dusty new asphalt usually means enormous lap-time gains across the weekend.

Overtaking picture

Unknown in practice. The layout is designed with long acceleration zones into slow corners to promote passing, but first-year circuits routinely disappoint — treat any confident prediction with scepticism on air.
