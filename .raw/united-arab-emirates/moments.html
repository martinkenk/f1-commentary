History in brief

  • First Grand Prix: 2009.
  • The day-to-night transition is the defining feature: qualifying conditions match the start of the race, not the end.
  • Both 2026 titles are settled before this finale; check the current standings for any remaining-place battles.
  • Haas confirmed Rafael Camara for the final two 2026 Grands Prix, including Abu Dhabi; this does not confirm an FP1 rookie outing.
  • Pirelli selected C3/C4/C5; its Abu Dhabi graphic marks C5 for Qualifying and C3/C4 for the Race.
  • 2010 — Vettel’s first title: Sebastian Vettel took pole and led from the start, while Fernando Alonso finished seventh after being caught behind Vitaly Petrov; Vettel clinched the championship by four points. Formula1.com title-decider retrospective.
  • 2021 — a one-lap title finish: After a late Safety Car, Max Verstappen passed Lewis Hamilton into Turn 5 on the final lap to win the race and his first World Championship. Formula1.com race highlights · official classification.
