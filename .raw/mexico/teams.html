Mexico City: team and title consequences

Antonelli seals the Drivers' title

Kimi Antonelli finished third and clinched the 2026 Drivers' Championship. At the post-race standings snapshot he had 380 points, ahead of Max Verstappen on 261 and Lewis Hamilton on 259. Mercedes leads Ferrari in the Constructors' standings, 630–504. Formula1.com race lowdown · official Drivers' standings · official Constructors' standings.

Verstappen wins from fifth

Max Verstappen took his sixth Mexico City Grand Prix victory, finishing 0.799 seconds ahead of Hamilton. The final classification gives Antonelli third and Leclerc fourth. Verstappen on the race-winning move · official race classification.

Post-race penalties changed the order

Leclerc's five seconds for leaving the track and gaining a lasting advantage left him fourth. Piastri's ten-second collision penalty dropped him to 13th in the final classification. Gasly received a stop-and-go for double yellows, while Colapinto received ten seconds for failing to slow sufficiently under a single yellow; both Alpines finished outside the points. Leclerc on his penalty · Piastri decision report · Alpine penalties · final classification.

The red flag reshaped tyre use

Pirelli reports Verstappen changed from Medium to Soft on lap 27, then took another Soft under the red flag. Antonelli ran his opening Soft stint to lap 43 before switching to Medium, then also used Soft after the stoppage. Pirelli said a one-stop would otherwise have been the most competitive option.
