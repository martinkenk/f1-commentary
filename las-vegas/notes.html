The circuit in one paragraph

A cold, late-night, very low-downforce blast down the Strip. Enormous top speeds and almost no corners of consequence — the whole weekend is defined by the struggle to get tyres into their working range on a cold street surface.

Numbers to have ready

  • Circuit length: 6.201km
  • Race: 50 laps, 309.958km
  • First Grand Prix here: 2023
  • Lap record: 1:34.876 — Oscar Piastri, 2024
  • Format: Standard weekend

Talking points

  • Track temperature falling through the session changes the tyre picture in real time.
  • Low-downforce trim exaggerates power-unit and deployment differences.

Name-check these corners

  • Turn 1 — Tight left immediately after the start; first-lap chaos point.
  • Turns 5–7 — The slow sequence before the Strip — traction out of here defines the long straight.
  • Turn 14 — End of the Strip; the heaviest braking zone and the primary overtaking place.
