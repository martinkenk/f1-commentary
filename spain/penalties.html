Published race-control watchlist

  • Turn 22 can affect both the current and following lap time in lap-time classified sessions.
  • Double-yellow sectors in free practice trigger lap-time deletion.
  • The narrow-pit-lane merge rule requires the released car to yield to existing fast-lane traffic when necessary.
  • The qualifying/reconnaissance SC2-to-SC1 maximum was set at below 1:42.0 between the Safety Car lines (FIA Document 22, 11 September).

FIA Competition Notes, Document 5, 10 September, and Document 22, 11 September. These are standing instructions, not penalties already imposed. Full circuit notes.

Verstappen's first-lap cut: Red Bull gave the places back

Verstappen kept his P3 starting position after cutting Turn 1 while racing Hamilton for it. Red Bull's Laurent Mekies explained that the team instructed him to give the places back to Antonelli and Hamilton on track to avoid a potential penalty. That team account is not evidence about whether the stewards opened an investigation; separately published FIA rulings appear in the automatic tracker below.
