4. Upgrades2026-09-18

Williams brings its long-awaited FW48 upgrade package to Baku

Williams will introduce a major, long-delayed upgrade package for the FW48 at the Azerbaijan Grand Prix after failing to score a point since Monaco. Drivers Alex Albon and Carlos Sainz say the package should bring a genuine step but caution it is unlikely to be the full gain calculated when it was signed off months ago, since rival midfield teams have kept developing in the meantime; team principal James Vowles calls the round a proof point for the team's processes ahead of the 2027 car (FW50).

Why it matters: This is a concrete, sourced upgrade claim tied specifically to this round, not a season-long generality — note it is expected to close the gap rather than move Williams into Q3 contention, and that both drivers have already tempered expectations publicly before the sessions.

Russell and Hamilton both concede the 2026 title fight after Madrid

Why it matters: Two of Antonelli's closest championship rivals publicly conceding narrows the on-air title framing heading into Baku to Antonelli versus a shrinking field; use the current standings table for the exact live gap rather than the 13 September figure.
