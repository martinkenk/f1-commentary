6 September: Antonelli wins from P19

Kimi Antonelli beat George Russell and Max Verstappen after starting 19th following his PU penalty. Norris beat Piastri to fourth; Hamilton finished sixth. Polesitter Gasly finished seventh, ahead of Lindblad, Colapinto and Tsunoda.

  1. Qualifying and grid: Gasly took pole ahead of Russell. Piastri qualified third but received a three-place grid penalty for impeding Lawson; Leclerc and Hamilton consequently shared the second row.
  2. Opening laps: Gasly retained the lead at the start; Russell passed him on lap 2. Hamilton lost ground in his first-chicane fight with Leclerc. Leclerc then crashed separately at Curva Alboreto on lap 2, bringing a red flag and barrier repairs. He climbed out unaided; that observation is not a medical diagnosis.
  3. Restart: Tsunoda's incorrect grid positioning caused an aborted start. Russell led the eventual restart; Verstappen and the recovering Antonelli worked through the order. Antonelli first took the lead on lap 18.
  4. Strategy split: Stroll's hydraulic retirement brought a Virtual Safety Car. Antonelli stopped for fresh mediums and Verstappen for hards; Russell stayed on the hards fitted during the red flag.
  5. Finish: after running through the gravel on lap 49, Antonelli recovered and passed Russell between Lesmo 2 and Ascari on lap 50. Alonso (suspected damage), Stroll (hydraulics) and Leclerc were the retirements.

Storylines going into FP1

Antonelli: home hero, title leader, recovery drive

  • Antonelli arrived for his home race after finishing second at Zandvoort.
  • His fifth ICE, fourth energy store and fourth control electronics brought a 30-place grid penalty (FIA Document 19).
  • Before this race, the last Italian winner of the Italian Grand Prix was Ludovico Scarfiotti in 1966.

Ferrari and the tifosi

  • Ferrari's special livery and race suits mark 30 years since Michael Schumacher joined the team.
  • The SF-26 gets Monza-specific low-drag changes across the floor, bodywork and rear wing.
  • Charles Leclerc won here in 2024; Ferrari need a home response after McLaren's back-to-back wins.

Four rookies in FP1

  • Luke Browning for Alex Albon at Williams.
  • Paul Aron for Pierre Gasly at Alpine.
  • Colton Herta for Sergio Perez at Cadillac.
  • Ayumu Iwasa for Max Verstappen at Red Bull.

Energy, not just minimum wing

  • Pirelli says simulations put 2026 lap times around two seconds faster than 2025.
  • Long straights make recharge and deployment a central performance constraint under the new power-unit rules.
  • McLaren expect a possible 'yo-yo effect' as cars harvest and deploy at different places around the lap.
