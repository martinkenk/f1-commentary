FP3 Turn 16 summons: no matching ruling listed

FIA Document 29 summoned Max Verstappen over alleged erratic driving at 16:33. The current 66-document event listing contains no separate matching ruling, so the outcome remains unresolved; a summons is not a finding or penalty. This is distinct from Document 37's Turn 14 warning and Document 38's separate Turn 17 no-further-action finding. Document 29 summons · FIA event document listing.

Arvid Lindblad summons followed by ruling

FIA Document 39 summoned the Racing Bulls team representative over an alleged unsafe release of Car 41 at 20:29. Document 45 later found that Lindblad's car was released into the path of Alex Albon's Car 23 and fined the team €5,000. The summons was an allegation, not the finding; the ruling and full report appear in the table below. Document 39 summons · Document 45 ruling.
