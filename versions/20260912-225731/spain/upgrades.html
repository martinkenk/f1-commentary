What changed and why

The explanations below summarise each team's stated rationale, not independently measured lap-time gains. A nil return means no updates submitted for this event; it does not rule out setup changes or previously introduced parts.

McLaren — 1 declared item

  • Rear wing: Additional rear-wing elements condition the airflow onto the mainplane and flap. Filed reason: Performance - Flow Conditioning.
View official declaration and diagram

Mercedes — 3 declared items

  • Rear wing: A shorter central winglet above the flap reduces local downforce and drag to suit Madrid's lift-to-drag requirements. Filed reason: Circuit specific - Drag Range.
  • Exhaust tailpipe: An extra winglet behind the exhaust turns the exhaust flow to generate load and drag in a ratio suited to Madrid. Filed reason: Circuit specific - Drag Range.
  • Front drum: A reprofiled front lip improves flow attachment across steering angles and the airflow reaching the rear of the car. Filed reason: Performance - Flow Conditioning.
View official declaration and diagram

Red Bull — 2 declared items

  • Rear corner: The more robust rear-wheel bodywork gaitor developed from Monza gains winglets behind the suspension fairings, aiming to recover earlier load while keeping the wheel bodywork sealed. Filed reason: Reliability.
  • Floor bib: Revised laminate and geometry between floor and chassis reduce local strain when the assembly deflects, aiming to prevent structural and aerodynamic-surface deterioration. Filed reason: Reliability.
View official declaration and diagram

Ferrari — 1 declared item

  • Rear suspension: The rearward leg fairing of the rear upper wishbone is reprofiled, adjusting incidence and spanwise loading for a local aerodynamic gain. Filed reason: Performance - Local Load.

Alpine — 1 declared item

  • Floor board: An extra element on the forward floor board changes pressure distribution and flow management to generate efficient local downforce. Filed reason: Performance - Local Load.

Cadillac — 2 declared items

  • Rear wing flap: A revised central trailing-edge winglet is reintroduced to increase rear load and improve aerodynamic stability across operating conditions. Filed reason: Performance - Local Load.
  • Diffuser vane: A small vertical turning vane on the inner trailing edge of the outboard diffuser sidewall improves outer-floor-channel performance and rear load. Filed reason: Performance - Local Load.
View official declaration and diagram

Friday car presentation: confirmed procedure

  • 12:00–13:00 Friday, Madrid local time (13:00–14:00 Tallinn).
  • One car from each team must be outside in its pit-stop position; the other must be available to view inside the garage.
  • If only one car carries major new aerodynamic/bodywork components intended for this event, that is the car which must be displayed to media.
  • The outside car may be used for pit-stop practice but must return to its display position when practice stops.
