What changed and why

The explanations below summarise each team's stated rationale, not independently measured lap-time gains. A nil return means no updates submitted for this event; it does not rule out setup changes or previously introduced parts.

McLaren — 1 declared item

  • Coke/engine cover: New high-cooling engine-cover bodywork increases cooling massflow for Sepang and upcoming hot events. Filed reason: Circuit specific - Cooling Range.
View official declaration and diagram

Mercedes — 7 declared items

  • Floor board: Reprofiled horizontal elements and a new slotted vertical upper element improve pressure distribution, reduce separation and feed the rear of the car. Filed reason: Performance - Local Load.
  • Floor leading edge: Reprofiled and additional downwashing elements increase local load and outwash, improving rear-floor flow. Filed reason: Performance - Flow Conditioning.
  • Floor corner: Rearranged slots and reprofiled elements improve local load and airflow into the diffuser. Filed reason: Performance - Flow Conditioning.
  • Floor body: Reprofiled diffuser roof, sidewall and winglet seek more load and robustness across the operating range. Filed reason: Performance - Local Load.
  • Rear suspension: Reprofiled track-rod and driveshaft fairings redistribute camber to work better with the diffuser winglet and rear-drum furniture. Filed reason: Performance - Local Load.
  • Rear corner: Revised rear-drum winglet span, chord and position complement the fairings and improve rear-tyre wake control. Filed reason: Performance - Local Load.
  • Rear bodywork: Reduced volume and a steeper side-view ramp increase downwash towards the rear floor and drum. Filed reason: Performance - Flow Conditioning.

  • Floor board: Revised geometry above the existing floor foot adds local load while preserving flow stability behind the front tyres. Filed reason: Performance - Local Load.

Mercedes package: the filing behind the development story

Formula1.com's 1 October report quotes Russell expecting Mercedes' Sepang upgrade package to help on more conventional circuits, after strong performance at Baku and Monza. Document 12 now confirms seven component rows spanning the floor, rear suspension, rear corner and rear bodywork. The intended benefits remain team claims, not an established lap-time gain.

Friday car display — confirmed procedure

The FIA sets 11:00–12:00 MYT on Friday 2 October (06:00–07:00 Tallinn): one car from each team at its pit-stop position, the other available inside the garage. If only one car carries the new major aero/bodywork components, that is the car that must be shown. This display procedure is distinct from the Car Presentation Submissions, which are now published and transcribed above from Document 12.
