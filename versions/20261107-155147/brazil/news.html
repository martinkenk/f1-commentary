1. Practice2026-11-07

Antonelli leads close FP2: the top five are within 0.321s

The official 21-car FP2 classification puts Kimi Antonelli fastest at 1:10.763, 0.253s ahead of Lando Norris; Max Verstappen (+0.259), George Russell (+0.273) and Oscar Piastri (+0.321) complete the top five. Nico Hülkenberg did not set a time. Formula1.com's Saturday Practice Debrief notes the fine margins among the leading teams and asks whether Mercedes can sustain its Friday pace. Stewards' Document 23 lists ten deleted-lap entries for nine drivers (Albon appears twice) after track-use issues at Turns 1 and 4; Race Director's Document 24 sets a below-1:13.0 interval between the Safety Car lines for qualifying and specified reconnaissance laps.

Why it matters: Antonelli leads the Friday sheet, but the top-five spread is only 0.321s and a single-lap classification does not establish race pace. FP3 is the next check on whether Mercedes can stay ahead. The lap-time ruling is not itself a revised classification or a race penalty. The 1:13.0 limit applies only during/after qualifying and on reconnaissance laps while pit exit is open, not to racing laps.

2. Stewards and grid2026-11-06

Russell gets five-place drop; Camara reprimanded after FP2

FIA Document 14 records Russell's sixth ICE against four allowed; the Stewards' separate Document 15 imposes a five-place grid drop. In Document 25, the Stewards found Rafael Camara unnecessarily impeded Charles Leclerc in FP2 and issued Camara his first driving reprimand of the season.

Why it matters: The rulings have different consequences: Russell's sanction affects his Brazil grid position; Camara's reprimand is not a grid penalty. Neither changes the FP2 classification.
