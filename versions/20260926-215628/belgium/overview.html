How the race went

  • Kimi Antonelli led from pole to flag for his first Spa victory and a big momentum boost at the top of the standings.
  • Charles Leclerc shadowed him home 1.9s back; Max Verstappen completed the podium ~11.6s adrift.
  • Lewis Hamilton (P4) and Oscar Piastri (P5) headed the chasing pack.
  • Lando Norris qualified P3 but a grid drop shuffled him back; he recovered to P7.

Why it matters

  • Antonelli extends his championship lead to 204 pts — 45 clear of Hamilton.
  • Mercedes stretch out front in the constructors' on 358 pts.
  • Ferrari's double score (Leclerc P2, Hamilton P4) keeps them second in both tables.
  • Next up: the Hungarian GP — a total contrast to Spa's power-track profile.
One-liner: Spa rewarded a clean, controlled drive — Antonelli never looked troubled once he held the lead into La Source. The story now is whether anyone can stop the Mercedes charge as the paddock heads to Budapest.
