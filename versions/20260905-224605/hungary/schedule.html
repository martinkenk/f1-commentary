Conditions to expect

Typical Hungaroring weather

  • Track temperatures are usually the highest of the season — peak European summer.
  • Heat drives thermal tyre degradation, especially on the rear axle.
  • History warns of the wildcard: Hungary's most dramatic races (2006, 2014, 2021) came when rain arrived.

Why it matters for FP1

  • Hot, low-grip surface means big lap-time evolution through the hour.
  • Teams will chase cooling and tyre-thermal balance early.
  • Watch cloud cover / wind — any threat of rain changes long-run plans instantly.
