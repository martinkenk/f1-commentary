1. Grid carry-over2026-11-22

Perez gets a five-place Qatar drop after the Vegas clash

Stewards' Document 61 found Perez wholly responsible for the Turn 14 collision with Rafael Câmara and converted his unserved 10-second penalty into five places at his next race. In a later Formula1.com report, Perez said he thought he was alongside; Câmara said he still needed to review what happened.

Why it matters: Apply the five places in Qatar grid preparation. Keep the drivers' accounts separate from the stewards' finding and do not copy Vegas power-unit penalties or energy limits into Qatar event data.

2. Tyre strategy and stint limit2026-11-25

Qatar's 27-lap cap, high pressures and conflicting set totals

Two Formula1.com articles published on 25 November give different per-driver allocations: the 10:05 UTC guide says 2 C1 Hard, 3 C2 Medium and 8 C3 Soft, with an extra Soft reserved for Q3; the 10:43 UTC explainer says each driver gets 13 sets (2 C1, 4 C2, 7 C3). Pirelli's 24 November release confirms the compounds and 27-lap cap, but not set totals. The FIA competition visa confirms 57 race laps over 5.419 km and a 19:00 local start. Its separate Pirelli competition notes prescribe C1 and C2 as mandatory race tyres, C3 for Q3, and 27 laps per set across tyre history; they do not state the conflicting per-driver allocation. The Race's 26 November report says the FIA notes set minimum starting pressures at 29 psi front and 27.5 psi rear, the season's highest front minimum. It attributes front-left overheating, wear and soft graining concerns to Williams, and the risk of an early stop forcing a third stop to McLaren. These are team assessments, not a predicted or published race strategy. Pirelli's reported counting rules include Safety Car and Virtual Safety Car laps; all race laps from start signal to chequered flag count, an unfinished lap in other sessions counts in full, mixed-mileage sets take the higher count, and reconnaissance, formation and post-race laps are excluded.

Why it matters: Keep the conflicting official F1 per-driver allocations explicitly attributed until clarified. The 29/27.5 psi figures are minimum starting pressures; the FIA table separately gives stabilized thresholds of 30/28.5 psi. Attribute the front-left and stop-timing assessments to the named teams, not as measured outcomes or official predicted strategy. Exact remaining-set counts still require a post-qualifying chart.

Sources: Pirelli, official Qatar tyre briefing and infographic, 24 November (graphic visually reviewed) / Pirelli official 2026 Qatar preview infographic (full image visually checked) / Formula1.com, Qatar tyre allocation and rules, 25 November / Formula1.com, 27-lap limit explainer quoting Pirelli, 25 November / The Race, tyre pressures and team strategy report, 26 November / FIA Qatar Competition Notes - Pirelli Preview, Doc 2, page 2 / FIA Qatar Competition Visa / Coffee Corner Motorsport, 24 November secondary preview (context only; no FIA counting rule inferred)

3. Championship and appeal2026-11-25

Both 2026 titles are decided; Tsunoda appeal remains pending

Kimi Antonelli clinched his first Drivers' Championship with third place in Mexico City on 1 November; Mercedes secured the Constructors' Championship in São Paulo on 8 November. After Las Vegas, Formula1.com lists Antonelli on 402 points to Max Verstappen's 304 and Mercedes on 666 to Ferrari's 517. Separately, the FIA scheduled a closed International Court of Appeal hearing in Paris for 25 November on Audi's appeal of Italian GP Document 69. The public judgments index, checked on 26 November, did not list a decision for ICA-2026-14; Las Vegas Document 66 also notes the appeal.

Why it matters: Qatar is not a title-deciding round. The appeal hearing notice does not itself change a race result or the points table; do not infer its outcome.

4. Haas line-up2026-11-26

Haas confirms Bearman for 2027; Câmara set for Qatar

TGR Haas confirmed on 26 November that Oliver Bearman has signed a new deal for 2027, completing next year's driver grid. Separately, Haas confirmed that Rafael Câmara will drive the final two 2026 Grands Prix and partner Bearman next season; Formula1.com reported that Freddie Slater will take Câmara's F2 seat for the final two rounds.

Why it matters: The 2027 Haas line-up is settled, but this announcement does not change the Qatar 2026 entry list. Câmara's Qatar participation is confirmed; his F2 replacement does not establish any additional FP1 substitutions.

5. Las Vegas fallout2026-11-26

Verstappen/Russell tension and Ferrari clash follow F1 to Qatar

Formula1.com's 25 November Qatar preview recounts the continuing Verstappen-Russell verbal dispute and Ferrari's Las Vegas fallout: Charles Leclerc hit team-mate Lewis Hamilton during their Turn 7 fight. FIA Document 60 found Leclerc wholly responsible and imposed a 10-second penalty; team principal Fred Vasseur described the incident as unacceptable. At Thursday's FIA press conference, Leclerc said Ferrari needed a solution, accepted responsibility for the Vegas clash, and declined to disclose whether the rules of engagement would change. That does not confirm new team rules or a Qatar ruling.

Why it matters: Keep the driver remarks attributed and the Ferrari ruling attached to its Las Vegas source; Leclerc's refusal to discuss possible rule changes is not an announcement that rules or team orders changed.

Sources: Formula1.com, Qatar race-week storylines, checked 25 November / FIA Las Vegas Document 60, visually reviewed / Formula1.com, Vasseur on the Ferrari collision / FIA Thursday press-conference transcript, 26 November

What the circuit demands

A fast, flowing, medium-to-high-speed loop originally built for motorcycles. Almost no slow corners and very high sustained lateral loads — physically brutal and historically the hardest race of the year on tyre construction.

Weekend format

Conventional weekend — three practice sessions, Qualifying and the Grand Prix.

  • All session times are shown in Lusail (AST) and Tallinn (EET).
  • Live weather is attached to each session on the Schedule & Weather page.
  • Session results appear on the Results page as soon as each session finishes.

Storylines to prepare

  • The FIA's Qatar power-unit map is published and reviewed: use its 1.0-second Overtake detection gap, and keep electrical Overtake separate from the four Straight Mode zones.
  • Sergio Perez carries a five-place grid drop from Las Vegas; keep that separate from Qatar's own event sanctions.

Overtaking picture

Moderate. The main straight offers a genuine chance, but the flowing middle sector makes following closely difficult and rewards clean air.
