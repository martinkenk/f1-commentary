The short version for São Paulo Grand Prix — everything worth having to hand.

The circuit in one paragraph

Short, anti-clockwise, hilly and permanently interesting. Barely 4.3 km, so the field is compressed and traffic is constant. Weather is famously volatile — sun and a downpour within the same hour is normal.

Numbers to have ready

  • Circuit length: 4.309km
  • Race: 71 laps, 305.879km
  • First Grand Prix here: 1973
  • Lap record: 1:10.540 — Valtteri Bottas, 2018
  • Format: Standard weekend

Talking points

  • Rain probability is high enough that a wet-weather plan is essential preparation.
  • Late-season championship maths often becomes live here.

Name-check these corners

  • Turns 1–2 — Senna S — Downhill left-hander at the end of the main straight; the classic overtaking spot and a lap-one flashpoint.
  • Turn 4 — Descida do Lago — Downhill left where cars can run side by side.
  • Subida dos Boxes — The long, climbing final sequence onto the pit straight — exit speed here sets up the next lap's pass.
