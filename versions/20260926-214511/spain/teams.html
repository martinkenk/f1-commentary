Team Watch & News

How each team's Madrid weekend actually went, plus the confirmed Red Bull-family substitutions.

How the weekend went for each team

  • Mercedes: Antonelli's eighth win of the season, helped by the VSC timing during pit stops; Russell finished P5 and has since said he is out of the title fight.
  • Ferrari: Hamilton retired early with a brake issue after first-lap contact with Verstappen; Leclerc ran long before finally pitting after 48 laps.
  • McLaren: Norris led from pole for most of the race but the VSC timing cost him the win outright; he finished third. Piastri was eighth.
  • Red Bull: Verstappen finished P2 after giving back a first-lap position gained by cutting Turn 1, on team orders from Laurent Mekies; Lawson was sixth.
  • Alpine / Racing Bulls: Colapinto was Alpine's sole scorer in P7; Lindblad took P9 for Racing Bulls, with Tsunoda P14 in his third weekend for the team.

Formula1.com race report; Mekies explains Verstappen instruction.

Confirmed race-driver replacements

  • Liam Lawson continues alongside Max Verstappen at Red Bull for a third weekend.
  • Yuki Tsunoda continues alongside Arvid Lindblad at Racing Bulls.
  • Isack Hadjar remains out while recovering from his wrist injury. The team says recovery is progressing and he will support them in Madrid; no return date is asserted here.
  • These are race-seat substitutions, not announcements of mandatory rookie FP1 outings.
