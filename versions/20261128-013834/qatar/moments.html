History in brief

  • First Grand Prix: 2021.
  • Run at night under lights, with big day-to-night track temperature swings.
  • Previous editions have required enforced maximum stint lengths on tyre-safety grounds.
  • Formula1.com 26 November race-week guide reports 41 overtakes in the 2025 race, but gives no counting methodology; keep that figure separate from the source-specific history above.
  • Formula1.com 26 November race-week guide reports Safety Car and Virtual Safety Car probabilities of 75% and 50%, respectively, based on the previous four Qatar races; these are small-sample historical frequencies, not a forecast for 2026.

2025: a Safety Car reshapes the title fight

With a 25-lap maximum tyre stint imposed for the 2025 race, Verstappen pitted under the early Safety Car while both McLarens stayed out. He won ahead of Piastri and Sainz; Norris finished fourth, taking a 12-point lead over Verstappen into the finale. That 25-lap cap was specific to 2025; Pirelli announced a separate 27-lap limit for each set in 2026. Formula1.com race report · 2026 Pirelli limit announcement.
