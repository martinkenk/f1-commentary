Race-weekend briefing — final facts

Headline: Verstappen won the 71-lap race from fifth on the grid; Antonelli finished third and secured the Drivers' title. The final winning margin over Hamilton was 0.799 seconds.

On-air talking points

  • Title: Antonelli became champion with third place; use the current standings page for the post-race points snapshot.
  • Tyres: Pirelli says the red flag changed the actual stints. Do not describe the pre-race one-stop forecast as the strategy every driver followed.
  • FP2 stewards: Docs 33–34 found that Max Verstappen and Liam Lawson made unnecessary, “clumsy” moves from the Turn 12 entry to the Turn 14 apex. Each received a one-place drop for the next race and one penalty point; their respective 12-month totals were one and four. Docs 23–24 were the summonses, not the penalties. Doc 33 · Doc 34 · Doc 23 · Doc 24.
  • Stewards: Leclerc's five seconds, Piastri's ten seconds, Gasly's stop-and-go and Colapinto's ten seconds are separate decisions with different incidents; the official final classification reflects them.
  • Classification: Ryo Hirakawa scored the final point in tenth for Haas; five entries are listed NC.
  • Pit-lane speeding: FIA Docs 18 and 25 fined Aston Martin €100 each after Fernando Alonso was measured at 80.2 km/h in FP1 and 80.1 km/h in FP2, against the 80 km/h limit. Doc 42 fined Cadillac €100 after Valtteri Bottas was measured at 80.9 km/h in FP3. Doc 18 · Doc 25 · Doc 42.
  • Driving standards: Stewards' Doc 54 clarified that on Lap 1 and the first lap after a resumption, cars in Turns 1–3 and 4–6 would be considered both attacking and defending; a lasting advantage from leaving the track could be avoided by yielding by Turn 4 or Turn 12, respectively. Official communication.
