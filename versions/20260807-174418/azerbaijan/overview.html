What the circuit demands

A contradiction: a 2.2 km flat-out blast along the seafront bolted onto a medieval old-town section barely wide enough for one car. Teams must choose between straight-line speed and the confidence to run close to the walls.

Weekend format

Conventional weekend — three practice sessions, Qualifying and the Grand Prix.

  • All session times are shown in Baku (AZT) and Tallinn (EEST).
  • Live weather is attached to each session on the Schedule & Weather page.

Storylines to prepare

  • Safety-car probability is high enough to be a genuine strategy input, not just a talking point.
  • The straight rewards efficient 2026 deployment — a good yardstick for power-unit form.

Overtaking picture

Excellent. The enormous DRS-assisted straight into a tight Turn 1 braking zone creates late lunges and slipstream chess. Low-drag cars can pass; high-downforce cars have to defend.
