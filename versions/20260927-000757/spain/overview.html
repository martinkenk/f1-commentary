Madrid's debut race: what actually happened

Kimi Antonelli won the Madring's first Grand Prix, beating Max Verstappen and pole-sitter Lando Norris. Norris led from pole until a Virtual Safety Car for Lance Stroll's stopped Aston Martin fell just after his own pit window closed, letting Antonelli and Verstappen pit far more cheaply and jump him. Lewis Hamilton retired early with a brake issue after first-lap contact with Verstappen, who was told to give back the place he gained by cutting Turn 1.

The race also produced very few on-track overtakes; The Race reports Madring organisers are already planning circuit changes for 2027, and F1's Sporting Advisory Committee is expected to discuss the VSC timing rules that decided the result.

Madrid: FIA facts that shaped the race

  • Two Straight Mode zones: after T22 and after T3. The separate Overtake detection is at entry to T22, with activation 20 m after T22.
  • Qualifying recharge: 7.5 MJ/lap, versus 9.0 MJ in free practice. Race recharge was 8.5 MJ without Overtake and 9.0 MJ with it.
  • C2 / C3 / C4 were the confirmed compounds. Slick starting pressures were 26.5 psi front / 25.5 psi rear in FIA/Pirelli Issue A.
  • T22 could cost two lap times in lap-time classified sessions; that rule did not change the race outcome, which turned on VSC pit-stop timing instead.

Confirmed race-driver replacements

  • Liam Lawson continues alongside Max Verstappen at Red Bull for a third weekend.
  • Yuki Tsunoda continues alongside Arvid Lindblad at Racing Bulls.
  • Isack Hadjar remains out while recovering from his wrist injury. The team says recovery is progressing and he will support them in Madrid; no return date is asserted here.
  • These are race-seat substitutions, not announcements of mandatory rookie FP1 outings.

Formula1.com / Red Bull announcement, 7 September 2026.

How the weekend went for each team

  • Mercedes: Antonelli's eighth win of the season, helped by the VSC timing during pit stops; Russell finished P5 and has since said he is out of the title fight.
  • Ferrari: Hamilton retired early with a brake issue after first-lap contact with Verstappen; Leclerc ran long before finally pitting after 48 laps.
  • McLaren: Norris led from pole for most of the race but the VSC timing cost him the win outright; he finished third. Piastri was eighth.
  • Red Bull: Verstappen finished P2 after giving back a first-lap position gained by cutting Turn 1, on team orders from Laurent Mekies; Lawson was sixth.
  • Alpine / Racing Bulls: Colapinto was Alpine's sole scorer in P7; Lindblad took P9 for Racing Bulls, with Tsunoda P14 in his third weekend for the team.

What the circuit demands

Brand new for 2026 — a part-street, part-permanent lap around the IFEMA exhibition grounds, including a banked corner. There is no previous Grand Prix here to benchmark: the FIA's operating limits are published, while measured degradation and strategy depend on this weekend's running.

Weekend format

Conventional weekend — three practice sessions, Qualifying and the Grand Prix.

Storylines to prepare

  • Anything can be called a 'first' here — first pole, first lap record, first winner.
  • Watch for track evolution: green, dusty new asphalt usually means enormous lap-time gains across the weekend.

Overtaking picture

Unknown in practice. The layout is designed with long acceleration zones into slow corners to promote passing, but first-year circuits routinely disappoint — treat any confident prediction with scepticism on air.
