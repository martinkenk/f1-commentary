Haas confirms Rafael Câmara for Qatar and Abu Dhabi

Haas confirmed on 22 November that Câmara will drive for the team at both remaining 2026 Grands Prix, after his race outings in Brazil and Las Vegas. The announcement also identifies him as a full-time Haas driver for 2027. FIA Document 11 lists 20 race entrants and separately names Jak Crawford as eligible to take part in FP1 under regulations B1.7.3 and B1.7.4; he is not a 21st race entrant. Formula1.com, 22 November · FIA entry list, page 2.

Verified Las Vegas carry-over

Sergio Perez: five-place grid penalty at his next race. FIA Las Vegas Document 61 finds him wholly responsible for contact with Rafael Câmara at Turn 14. Because he retired and could not serve the time penalty, it becomes a grid drop at the next race in which he participates, expected to be Qatar. This is not a five-second Qatar penalty. Original Stewards' ruling, 21 November.

Las Vegas PU penalties for Antonelli, Piastri, Sainz, Stroll and Bearman belong to that event; do not carry them forward again. Reviewed Vegas technical and sanction tables. Qatar's own energy limits, tyre prescriptions and upgrades must come from Qatar publications, not copied Las Vegas figures. What the circuit demands

A fast, flowing, medium-to-high-speed loop originally built for motorcycles. Almost no slow corners and very high sustained lateral loads — physically brutal and historically the hardest race of the year on tyre construction.

Weekend format

Conventional weekend — three practice sessions, Qualifying and the Grand Prix.

  • All session times are shown in Lusail (AST) and Tallinn (EET).
  • Live weather is attached to each session on the Schedule & Weather page.
  • Session results appear on the Results page as soon as each session finishes.

Weekend storylines

  • The FIA's Qatar power-unit map is published and reviewed: use its 1.0-second Overtake detection gap, and keep electrical Overtake separate from the four Straight Mode zones.
  • Sergio Perez carries a five-place grid drop from Las Vegas; keep that separate from Qatar's own event sanctions.

Overtaking picture

Moderate. The main straight offers a genuine chance, but the flowing middle sector makes following closely difficult and rewards clean air.

Qatar FIA filing: Audi declares one front-corner update

FIA Document 12 lists one declared item across all eleven teams: Audi's revised lower front brake-duct deflector, filed for performance / flow conditioning. The description says it improves flow-field quality delivered to the rest of the car; this is the team's stated rationale, not an independently measured gain. Ten teams explicitly submitted no updates for Qatar. Audi's component-location diagram is on page 11. Audi declaration, page 10 · component diagram, page 11 · Full team-by-team filing and readers.
