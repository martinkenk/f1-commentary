Great Moments

Madring's first Grand Prix moment now has a name: Kimi Antonelli's Madrid win.

A new venue, not Barcelona or Jarama

Before its 13 September 2026 debut, no Formula 1 Grand Prix had been held at the Madring. Past winners, polesitters, race lap records and current-grid starts or best finishes at this circuit are therefore not applicable before its debut, not missing historical research. Simulator laps and Formula 3 testing are not F1 race records.

Madrid's earlier Grands Prix were at Jarama, first used in 1968 and last used for a World Championship Grand Prix in 1981. Those are different-track history; neither Jarama nor Barcelona results should be relabelled as Madring records.

Formula1.com circuit guide, 10 September 2026.

The circuit's first chapter, now written

Kimi Antonelli won the Madring's inaugural Grand Prix on 13 September 2026, beating Max Verstappen and pole-sitter Lando Norris after a Virtual Safety Car reshuffled the pit-stop order. Lando Norris took the circuit's first pole position the day before. Full official classifications are on Results, and the race narrative is on Weekend News.
