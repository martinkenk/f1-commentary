FIA Doc 3

Power Unit & Override

The FIA's official power-and-energy map for Zandvoort — Doc 3, published 19 Aug 2026.

FP & Qualifying energy limits, called out: the maximum recharge per lap is 9.0 MJ in Free Practice but only 7.5 MJ in Sprint Qualifying & Qualifying — a full 1.5 MJ tighter once it matters for grid position, which is exactly why Saturday one-lap pace can look different from what Friday's single practice hour suggested.

Overtake system (Article B7.2)

  • Detection gap: 1.0 s.
  • Detection line: 3431 m into the lap (lap-distance loop L20).
  • Activation line: 3648 m into the lap (loop L21) — on the run toward Turn 10/11.
  • Maximum PU power-reduction rate once power-limited: 100 kW/s over a 2411 m power-limited distance.

Where the exceptions apply

  • T1–T3 (350–800 m), T8–T10 (2000–2450 m) and T11–T13 (3100–3400 m) are the sectors where a Power Limited Pending reduction may exceed 150 kW (up to 350 kW).
  • A reset of MGU-K power reduction is permitted on the exit of Turn 14 (3700–4200 m).
  • Outside Sprint & Race, every session (including Qualifying) runs the more permissive “Base – Overtake” power curve rather than the race-day standard map.

What changed for 2026

  • Roughly a 50/50 split between internal combustion and electrical power.
  • The MGU-H is gone; the MGU-K is substantially more powerful.
  • 100% sustainable fuel.
  • Manual Override replaces DRS as the overtaking aid — extra energy the chasing driver can call on inside the detection/activation window above.

Honda 'Spec 2' at Zandvoort

  • Honda have detailed an updated power unit for Aston Martin, intended to debut this weekend.
  • It follows recent chassis upgrades as the team tries to climb clear of the back of the field.
  • Watch the Power Unit page & Team Watch for how it lands across FP1/qualifying trim.
