Pocket bios

Leonardo Fornaroli — McLaren

  • Italian, 21. 2025 F2 champion; McLaren Driver Development Programme & reserve.
  • 2nd career FP1 outing — debut at Barcelona last month (in Norris' car); now takes over Piastri's.
  • Also has laps in older McLaren and Haas machinery.

Frederik Vesti — Mercedes

  • Danish. Mercedes' sole reserve for 2026 after 5+ years in the programme.
  • 6th F1 practice outing (five prior since 2023); now racing IMSA sportscars.
  • Faces the pressure of a title contender's car (as in Barcelona).

Paul Aron — Alpine

  • Estonian, 22. Third in the 2024 F2 championship.
  • 8th FP1 outing — debut with Sauber at the 2025 British GP, then Hungary, Monza, Mexico City & Abu Dhabi, plus Audi runs at Barcelona & Austria this year.
  • First appearance for Alpine in 2026; supports Gasly and Colapinto — the latter steps aside in FP1.

Ryo Hirakawa — Haas

  • Japanese, 32. Haas test & reserve driver; races WEC with Toyota.
  • 7th career FP1 outing — across 3 teams (McLaren, Alpine, Haas).
  • 2nd FP1 of 2026 (after Austria), taking Bearman's car.

Colton Herta — Cadillac

  • American, 26. Cadillac test driver.
  • 2nd career FP1 outing — debut at Barcelona (in Perez' car); now into Bottas' MAC-26.
  • Extensive IndyCar experience; moved to F2 for 2026 (16th, 26 pts). Two more practice runs due this year after Hungary.

Also worth a mention

  • Jak Crawford (Aston Martin reserve, F2 runner-up 2025) has been central to the AMR26 upgrade sim work.
  • Arvid Lindblad (Racing Bulls) — full rookie racing this year, already trading blows with Lawson.
