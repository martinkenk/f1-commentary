What changed and why

The explanations below summarise each team's stated rationale, not independently measured lap-time gains. A nil return means no updates submitted for this event; it does not rule out setup changes or previously introduced parts.

McLaren — 2 declared items

  • Floor Furniture: New floor board and floor edge target floor loading. Filed reason: Performance - Flow Conditioning.
  • Floor Furniture: Rear floor furniture changes condition diffuser flow. Filed reason: Performance - Flow Conditioning.
View official declaration and diagram

Mercedes — 4 declared items

  • Rear Wing: Removed winglet devices reduce drag for Las Vegas. Filed reason: Circuit specific - Drag Range.
  • Front Bodywork: Trimmed mirror rear stay reduces camber and loading. Filed reason: Circuit specific - Drag Range.
  • Rear Corner: Repositioned rear-corner winglets target the local load requirement. Filed reason: Performance - Local Load.
  • Engine Cover: Narrower rear exit matches the event cooling requirement. Filed reason: Cooling Range.

McLaren's Las Vegas package is now documented

The FIA car-presentation filing lists two McLaren floor-furniture items: a new floor board and a floor-edge change, both described as performance flow-conditioning work. Andrea Stella's earlier interview previewed more parts for Las Vegas but did not identify them. That interview is a team statement and was not an FIA filing. The event filing is evidence of declared parts, not proof of a measured lap-time gain. FIA team submissions · Formula1.com, 9 November.
