1. Championship2026-11-27

Titles are settled; Hamilton and Red Bull lead close fights for third

The pre-Qatar Formula1.com tables after Las Vegas show Hamilton on 267 points, three ahead of Russell and Norris on 264 and 17 ahead of Leclerc on 250. Red Bull hold third in the Constructors' table on 440, nine ahead of McLaren on 431. Antonelli and Mercedes have already secured their championships; these are pre-Qatar standings and should be refreshed after that race.

Why it matters: Abu Dhabi is a season finale, not a title decider. Keep the settled championships separate from the remaining-position battles and update the live points before the final round.

2. Haas line-up2026-11-26

Camara is confirmed for Abu Dhabi; Bearman's 2027 seat is settled

Haas confirmed that Rafael Camara will drive its final two 2026 Grands Prix, including Abu Dhabi. On 26 November, Formula1.com reported Camara's decision to leave his F2 title challenge for the final two rounds and Haas's confirmation that Oliver Bearman will stay for 2027, completing next year's grid.

Why it matters: Camara's Abu Dhabi race seat is confirmed; this does not establish an FP1 rookie appearance. Keep the 2027 contract update distinct from the 2026 weekend entry.

Sources: Formula1.com: Haas confirms Camara for the final 2026 races, 22 November / Formula1.com: Camara reacts to leaving the F2 title challenge, 26 November / Formula1.com: Bearman continues with Haas in 2027, 26 November

What the circuit demands

The season finale, run into the dusk so that track temperature drops steadily through the race. Reprofiled in 2021 into a faster, more flowing lap with banked corners; still fundamentally a traction-and-braking circuit.

Weekend format

Conventional weekend — three practice sessions, Qualifying and the Grand Prix.

  • All session times are shown in Abu Dhabi (GST) and Tallinn (EET).
  • Live weather is attached to each session on the Schedule & Weather page.
  • Session results appear on the Results page as soon as each session finishes.

Storylines to prepare

  • Both 2026 titles are settled; use the live standings to identify the final positions still mathematically open.
  • Haas confirmed Rafael Camara for Abu Dhabi; FP1 rookie entries remain a separate question.
  • Pirelli selected C3/C4/C5; the nomination graphic is not a pressure-and-camber preview.
  • Falling temperatures make the second half of the race quicker; expect late strategy gambles.

Overtaking picture

Improved since the reprofile but still not easy; the long straights into heavy braking zones are where it happens, helped by two DRS zones.
