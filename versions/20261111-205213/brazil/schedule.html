Standard weekend, not Sprint: Friday FP1 12:30 and FP2 16:00; Saturday FP3 11:30 and Qualifying was scheduled for 15:00; the Race started Sunday at 14:00 (all BRT). Add five hours for Tallinn (EET): Race 19:00 Sunday 8 November. FIA Timetable V5 supersedes V4 and extends the third Brazil Junior Trophy race from 20 to 25 minutes plus one lap (08:50–09:20 BRT). The Grand Prix started at 14:00 BRT; the official classification records 71 laps and 305.879 km in 1:35:23.504. Timetable V5, page 2. The Race reports that Qualifying was delayed by 1h25 and red-flagged almost immediately in heavy rain. Curfew report, 7 November: FIA Technical Delegate Document 27 says Oracle Red Bull Racing and Aston Martin personnel were inside the circuit during the 11.5-hour pre-FP3 curfew, from 21:00 Friday to 08:30 Saturday BRT. This was Oracle Red Bull Racing's second and Aston Martin's third of four permitted team exceptions; the report says no action should be taken. FIA Document 27.

Qualifying parc-fermé deadline: FIA Race Director Document 47, issued at 18:23 on Saturday, required every car used in Qualifying to be covered and ready for FIA seals by 19:27 local time under Regulation B3.4.3a. FIA Document 47.

FIA Media Delegate's Document 29 — Post-Qualifying Procedure: if the top three take the chequered flag at the end of Q3, they return to the pit lane at the 1–3 boards, then are weighed by the FIA in full race gear. Post-qualifying interviews take place in Parc Fermé; access to the designated area is limited to mechanics, officials and pre-approved media, not driver physios or team PR. The top three then complete the photo, press-conference and TV-pen sequence. Drivers eliminated in Q1/Q2, those classified 4th–10th in Q3 and eligible drivers who did not participate in Q1 must attend the required TV-pen interviews immediately after weighing; drivers must not drink or increase their weight before it is recorded. Top-ten drivers report directly to the FIA scales on leaving their cars, and drivers who stop on track (unless sent to the Medical Centre) are weighed before returning to their teams. The parc-fermé layout is on page 4. FIA Document 29, procedure · parc-fermé diagram · complete FIA event listing.

Race-day anthem procedure: The FIA Media Delegate's 8 November pre-race procedure said drivers should move to the anthem position at 13:40 BRT and be in place by 13:42; the national anthem was scheduled for 13:44, with drivers in race suits. It reiterates that all drivers must be at the front of the grid no less than 16 minutes before the scheduled formation lap. The accompanying diagram shows the anthem setup; these are instructions, not a report of compliance. FIA pre-race procedure, page 2 · anthem-position diagram, page 3.

Post-race procedure: The FIA Media Delegate's 8 November procedure directs the top three to return to the Pit Lane after the chequered flag, where the 1–3 boards are at the entrance, then be weighed by the FIA in full race attire; water is available after weighing. Their post-race interviews take place in Parc Fermé before the cooldown-room and podium sequence. Drivers classified fourth or lower proceed to the TV pen after weighing; the document also covers media commitments for retirees. Its final page diagrams the Parc Fermé and race podium layout. This is procedure, not a penalty or confirmation that any individual complied. FIA post-race procedure, pages 2–3 · Parc Fermé diagram, page 4.
