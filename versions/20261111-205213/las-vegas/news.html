1. Drivers' Championship2026-11-01

Antonelli clinched the 2026 Drivers' Championship in Mexico City

Third place in the Mexico City Grand Prix was enough for Kimi Antonelli to secure his maiden Drivers' title, regardless of where his rivals finished.

Why it matters: The title is already decided before Las Vegas; do not present the current standings gap as an active championship fight.

Sources: Formula1.com on Antonelli's title

2. Constructors' Championship2026-11-08

Mercedes clinched the 2026 Constructors' title in São Paulo

George Russell finished fourth and Kimi Antonelli fifth in the São Paulo Grand Prix, enough for Mercedes to secure its ninth Constructors' Championship.

Why it matters: Both championship titles are settled. Las Vegas is about race wins and the remaining finishing positions, not title permutations.

Sources: Formula1.com on the São Paulo race and Mercedes' title

3. McLaren development2026-11-09

Stella says further McLaren parts are planned for Las Vegas

Stella said cooler track and ambient temperatures helped the MCL40 control tyre temperatures in Brazil, while hot-condition long-run consistency remains work in progress. He said the Interlagos upgrades will be complemented by further parts in Las Vegas as a foundation for 2027; he named no components.

Why it matters: This is a team development plan, not a published FIA submission or a measured performance gain. The Las Vegas FIA listing remains inaccessible, so the component filing status is unverified.

Sources: Formula1.com on Stella's McLaren development plan
