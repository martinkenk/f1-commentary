FIA resolves 2025 financial-regulation cases for Williams and Alpine

The FIA's 25 November 2026 review covered 11 team submissions: the 10 teams that raced in 2025 plus newcomer Cadillac. Nine were compliant with the 2025 financial regulations; Williams had a minor overspend and procedural breach, while Alpine had a procedural breach. All five power-unit manufacturers were compliant. The Williams agreement says the unintentional overspend was £627,000 (0.48%) and found no sporting advantage. Its 12-month sporting sanction subtracts 1.5 percentage points from the coefficient used to set restricted wind-tunnel and CFD allowances (for example, 90% becomes 88.5%); this is not a Qatar grid or race penalty. The Alpine agreement requires a financial penalty and investigation costs, but no sporting penalty. The public summaries do not state either financial-penalty amount.
