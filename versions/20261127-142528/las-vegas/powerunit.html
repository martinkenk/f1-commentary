Las Vegas FIA power and energy map

FIA Power Unit Information: 6.201 km, standard format, 19–21 November. Maximum recharge per lap is 8.0 MJ in the race without Overtake, 8.5 MJ with Overtake, 6.0 MJ in qualifying, 8.5 MJ in free practice and 9.0 MJ on out laps other than in the race. These are recharge limits, not deployment allowances.

Article C5.12.8: power-limited distance 4448 m, reduction-rate limit 50 kW/s. Main overtaking-zone curves are Base–Standard without Overtake and Base–Overtake with it; elsewhere the race uses Alt 1. Practice including qualifying uses Base–Overtake. The complete curves and exception windows remain in the original diagram below.

Alternative-curve sectors: T1–T3, 300–500 m; T5–T12, 1520–3150 m. Power-reduction exceptions permit 350 kW at T1–T3 (300–500 m), T7–T9 (2000–2200 m), T10–T11 (2670–3100 m), plus [Exit T14, 5100–5700 m]. Reset windows: Exit T3, 540–800 m; [Exit T16, 5700–6200 m]. Brackets apply only in SQ/Q as the source specifies; they do not establish a Sprint session at this standard-format event. Higher-speed thresholds are 240 km/h at T3–T4 (450–620 m) and 310 km/h at T10–T12 (2550–3100 m).

Overtake: 1.0-second detection gap, detection at 5299 m / L19, activation at 5400 m / L20. Loop labels are not turn numbers. Straight Mode is active aero, separate from the Overtake energy system; do not describe the map's SM markings as extra Overtake activation lines.

FP3 and qualifying scrutineering — FIA Document 47

The Technical Delegate's five-page report records checks across both sessions, including fuel flow, ERS recharge, engine and MGU-K limits, car software and aerodynamic components. All listed items were found compliant with the 2026 Technical Regulations. This is a compliance report, not a component-use update or penalty. Complete FIA report.

FP1 and FP2 scrutineering — FIA Document 24

The four-page Technical Delegate report records checks of exhaust declarations, tyre pressures, fuel-flow and engine limits, ERS recharge, MGU-K, software and control systems across both practice sessions. All listed items were found compliant; the report is not a component-use update or penalty. Complete FIA report.

Approved parc fermé replacements

In Document 52, 21 November, the Technical Delegate lists approved replacements and parameter changes across several cars. Leclerc's Car 16 received previously used energy-store and control-electronics elements and a new intercooler water pump, with associated parameter changes. The report says the listed work was approved after written team requests; it is not itself a penalty decision or an updated season-wide usage table.
