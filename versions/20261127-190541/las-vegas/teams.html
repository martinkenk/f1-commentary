Team Watch & News

Team-by-team storylines for the weekend.

Las Vegas race result

Max Verstappen won, with Lando Norris second and Oscar Piastri third. These are the completed Las Vegas results, not a forecast for the next round. Official race classification.

McLaren's Las Vegas package is now documented

The FIA car-presentation filing lists two McLaren floor-furniture items: a new floor board and a floor-edge change, both described as performance flow-conditioning work. Andrea Stella's earlier interview previewed more parts for Las Vegas but did not identify them. That interview is a team statement and was not an FIA filing. The event filing is evidence of declared parts, not proof of a measured lap-time gain. FIA team submissions · Formula1.com, 9 November.

Red Bull's Las Vegas front-wing update

Formula1.com's 24 November technical report says Red Bull ran a new front-wing endplate to complement earlier sidepod changes and compared endplate versions while working on Max Verstappen's car balance. The FIA filing lists one front-wing-endplate declaration and describes a lower, shorter dive-plane intended to protect downstream flow stability. This is technical context, not evidence of a measured lap-time gain. Formula1.com report · FIA team submissions.
