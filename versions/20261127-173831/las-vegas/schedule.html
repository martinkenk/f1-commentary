FIA procedure and compliance documents

Post-qualifying procedure: the top three drivers had to weigh immediately after the session, remain fully attired and attend the FIA interviews and podium media commitments in the prescribed order. Cars that stop during qualifying must proceed through the FIA scales through the pit lane. FIA post-qualifying procedure.

Pre-race procedure: all drivers moved to the national-anthem position no later than 19:42 and remained attired in the pit lane until 19:42. The procedure is an instruction, not a report of compliance. FIA pre-race procedure.

Post-race procedure: the top three weighed after the chequered flag, took the podium in 1st/2nd/3rd order and were escorted back to the pit lane and press conference. The procedure is not evidence of compliance or an individual ruling. FIA post-race procedure.

Curfew and weekend forms: the official timetable, corrected covers-on-time report, curfew report, self-scrutineering declaration, competition visa and car-display procedure remain linked source records. The Friday display ran 15:00–16:00 local at one team's pit-stop position, with only that car shown and major aerodynamic/bodywork components unrun before display. Timetable · Curfew · Corrected covers-on-time report · Car display · Self-scrutineering · Competition visa.

Race-control V2: the revised competition notes retain 23:02 as the Qualifying lap-time car-line deadline and confirm the race lapping, blue-cockpit-light and marshalling-system rules. FIA competition notes · FIA competition notes V2.
