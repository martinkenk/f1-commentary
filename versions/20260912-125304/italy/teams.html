Team Watch & News

Race outcome first; the dated pre-event team briefing is retained for context.

6 September: Antonelli wins from P19

Kimi Antonelli beat George Russell and Max Verstappen after starting 19th following his PU penalty. Norris beat Piastri to fourth; Hamilton finished sixth. Polesitter Gasly finished seventh, ahead of Lindblad, Colapinto and Tsunoda.

  1. Qualifying and grid: Gasly took pole ahead of Russell. Piastri qualified third but received a three-place grid penalty for impeding Lawson; Leclerc and Hamilton consequently shared the second row.
  2. Opening laps: Gasly retained the lead at the start; Russell passed him on lap 2. Hamilton lost ground in his first-chicane fight with Leclerc. Leclerc then crashed separately at Curva Alboreto on lap 2, bringing a red flag and barrier repairs. He climbed out unaided; that observation is not a medical diagnosis.
  3. Restart: Tsunoda's incorrect grid positioning caused an aborted start. Russell led the eventual restart; Verstappen and the recovering Antonelli worked through the order. Antonelli first took the lead on lap 18.
  4. Strategy split: Stroll's hydraulic retirement brought a Virtual Safety Car. Antonelli stopped for fresh mediums and Verstappen for hards; Russell stayed on the hards fitted during the red flag.
  5. Finish: after running through the gravel on lap 49, Antonelli recovered and passed Russell between Lesmo 2 and Ascari on lap 50. Alonso (suspected damage), Stroll (hydraulics) and Leclerc were the retirements.

Pre-event team briefing archive

Mercedes

  • Antonelli's full power-unit change is strategic rather than the result of a new failure.
  • Monza was selected because passing opportunities reduce the likely cost of starting at the back.
  • See Championship & Form for the latest sourced standings and championship gaps.

Ferrari

  • Home-race Schumacher tribute livery and race suits.
  • Low-drag floor/bodywork adaptation and removal of rear-wing hanger flaps; the FTM exhaust wing stays.
  • A new ADUO 2 combustion-engine specification is ready, but its Monza introduction was not confirmed at publication.

McLaren

  • Norris arrives with consecutive pole-to-win weekends in Hungary and Zandvoort.
  • New low-drag rear wing, first use of the rotating H-Wing, and smaller aero options to evaluate.
  • Energy harvesting and deployment are expected to produce pace swings around the lap.
  • Pre-event team-mate context: Piastri's previous win was Zandvoort 2025; the current points comparison is on Championship & Form.

Red Bull family

  • Lawson continues in Hadjar's Red Bull seat; Tsunoda continues at Racing Bulls.
  • Iwasa takes Verstappen's car for FP1.
  • Lawson scored seventh on short notice at Zandvoort and now gets a conventional three-practice weekend.

Alpine

  • Both Gasly and Colapinto receive the upgrade package first raced on Gasly's car at Zandvoort.
  • Aron uses FP1 to compare that package against his simulator work.

Williams & Cadillac

  • Browning replaces Albon at Williams for FP1.
  • Herta replaces Perez at Cadillac and samples the MAC-26 in low-drag trim for the first time.

Aston Martin

  • CTO Enrico Cardile says the AMR26's aero development is essentially done — no more upgrades planned beyond a stray part worth track-testing for 2027.
  • Weight-saving programme continues, targeted for completion by the Azerbaijan round.
  • Honda's Zandvoort Spec 2 engine (an estimated 10–30bhp step) is its last major 2026 hardware update; Alonso called it "not a big improvement, to be honest".
