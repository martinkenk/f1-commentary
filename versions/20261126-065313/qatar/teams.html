Team Watch & News

Team-by-team storylines for the weekend.

Haas confirms Rafael Câmara for Qatar and Abu Dhabi

Haas confirmed on 22 November that Câmara will drive for the team at both remaining 2026 Grands Prix, after his race outings in Brazil and Las Vegas. The announcement also identifies him as a full-time Haas driver for 2027. This confirms Câmara's Qatar participation; it is not a complete FIA entry list or confirmation of FP1 substitutions. Formula1.com, 22 November.

FIA resolves 2025 financial-regulation cases for Williams and Alpine

The FIA's 25 November 2026 review covered 11 team submissions: the 10 teams that raced in 2025 plus newcomer Cadillac. Nine were compliant with the 2025 financial regulations; Williams had a minor overspend and procedural breach, while Alpine had a procedural breach. All five power-unit manufacturers were compliant. The Williams agreement says the unintentional overspend was £627,000 (0.48%) and found no sporting advantage. Its 12-month sporting sanction subtracts 1.5 percentage points from the coefficient used to set restricted wind-tunnel and CFD allowances (for example, 90% becomes 88.5%); this is not a Qatar grid or race penalty. The Alpine agreement requires a financial penalty and investigation costs, but no sporting penalty. The public summaries do not state either financial-penalty amount. FIA's 2025 compliance review · Williams accepted breach agreement · Williams public summary · Alpine accepted breach agreement · Alpine public summary · Formula1.com report.

Ferrari's Las Vegas tension carries into Qatar

Formula1.com's 25 November Qatar preview also revisits the Ferrari collision in Las Vegas: Charles Leclerc hit team-mate Lewis Hamilton during their Turn 7 fight. FIA Document 60 found Leclerc wholly responsible and imposed a 10-second penalty; team principal Fred Vasseur described the incident as unacceptable. These are Las Vegas developments, not Qatar sanctions. The separate Hamilton Doc 54 / final Doc 65 footnote discrepancy remains unresolved on the Las Vegas pages. Formula1.com Qatar preview · FIA Las Vegas Document 60 · Vasseur's post-race comments · FIA Document 54 · FIA final Document 65.

Bearman's 2027 Haas seat: expectation, not a formal announcement

Formula1.com's 25 November Qatar preview says Haas had not yet formally announced Oliver Bearman's 2027 seat. Team Principal Ayao Komatsu had said there was no doubt Bearman would drive for Haas, while noting that the contract was still being finalised. Treat the expected seat and a signed announcement as distinct. Formula1.com Qatar preview · Komatsu on Bearman's seat.
