Circuit notes

  • Zandvoort's first World Championship Grand Prix was in 1952; it returned in 2021 after a 36-year absence.
  • The 2026 race is scheduled to be one of the venue's last under its current deal — expect plenty of farewell framing.
  • Grandstands are famously orange and the crowd noise is a genuine broadcast feature.

I’ve only done this one on a bike! Zandvoort is actually a lovely circuit that has been adapted really well for modern F1. It’s still difficult to overtake on… but the banking makes a huge difference. You don’t really appreciate the banking until you go around it at Turn 3… It is a nice, flowing track. You’ve got to have commitment, particularly in the middle sector, which is undulating… because it’s a punishing circuit as well.
— Jolyon Palmer, former Renault F1 driver
