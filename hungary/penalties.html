Penalties & Stewards

Every stewards' decision, infringement, fine and penalty from the FIA Hungarian GP event documents.

Two front-runners hit. Hamilton takes a 3-place grid penalty for impeding Piastri (P2 → P5) and Antonelli a 3-place penalty plus a penalty point for not slowing under yellows (P4 → P7) — promoting Piastri to P3 and Verstappen to P4. Antonelli also picked up a warning for a slow in-lap. Add the earlier €400 fine (Hadjar), a Sainz warning and two FP1 "no further action" rulings; track limits policed hard all weekend. Post-qualifying, Perez is sent to the pit lane for a parc-fermé suspension change (FIA Doc 50).
